## 3. Buyer Personas & Psychological Pain Points

### Persona A: Local Sole Trader & Tradesperson (Tier 1 Focus)
- **Target Profiles:** Plumbers, electricians, landscapers, builders across East Sussex.
- **Pain Points:** Lost evenings/weekends to paperwork, tax stress (Self-Assessment deadline fear), lack of professional web presence to attract affluent local clients.
- **TotalBiz Solution:** Turnkey "Getting You Online" package + Digital Admin & Invoicing setup.

### Persona B: Small Business Owner / Commercial Office (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Focus)
- **Target Profiles:** High Street retailers, local accountants, solicitors, growing 5–20 person teams.
- **Pain Points:** Outgrown startup workflows, staff confusion, dropping Wi-Fi during client calls, cannot afford a £249k/yr full-time COO.
- **TotalBiz Solution:** Blended Fractional COO / TOM advisory + Mesh Wi-Fi & physical cabling stabilization.

### Persona C: Airbnb Host & Rental Landlord (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Focus)
- **Target Profiles:** Property investors & short-term rental owners in Sussex & UK-wide.
- **Pain Points:** Fear of 1-star reviews from lost keys, Wi-Fi drops, high heating bills left on by guests.
- **TotalBiz Solution:** Smart Home lock installation + Mesh Wi-Fi + **Proprietary GCloud Dynamic Pricing Engine** & Co-hosting services.

### Persona D: Busy Professional & Remote Home Worker (Tier 1 Focus)
- **Target Profiles:** Corporate executives, consultants relocated to rural Sussex.
- **Pain Points:** Embarrassing Zoom dropouts through thick stone walls, poor home office backup protocols.
- **TotalBiz Solution:** Mesh Wi-Fi dead-spot resolution + Tech fixes + Smart Home routines.
