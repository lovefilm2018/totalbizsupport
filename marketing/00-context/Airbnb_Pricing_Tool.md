# Airbnb Co-Hosting & Automated Dynamic Pricing Engine

## Proprietary Asset Overview
- **Product/Service:** Automated Airbnb & Short-Term Rental Dynamic Pricing Engine + Co-Hosting Services.
- **Creator:** Developed by Alex Poxon (GCloud daily job / webhook scraper).
- **Functionality:** 
  - Automatically scrapes/monitors competitor nightly rates across Airbnb and Booking.com for the next 60 days.
  - Detects local market availability drops / demand spikes (e.g. competitor rate jumping from £100 to £400 for event weekends).
  - Triggers automated webhook alerts suggesting optimal price increases or reductions.
- **Current Live Proof:** Actively powering pricing for **Bijou Coastal Stays** (Worthing, West Sussex).

## Target Audience & Growth Vector
- **Primary Prospects:** Property owners, Airbnb hosts, and short-term rental landlords in East & West Sussex and UK-wide.
- **Pain Point:** Most hosts are non-technical real estate investors who set static rates or attempt manual pricing checks, missing thousands in peak revenue or overpricing during quiet periods. Existing tools (PriceLabs, Wheelhouse) are complex and confusing for average hosts.
- **Marketing Channel:** Airbnb Co-Host Network direct messaging, local property owner groups, targeted LinkedIn/Facebook content.
- **Offer Positioning:** *"Done-for-You Airbnb Revenue Optimization & Hands-off Co-Hosting."*
