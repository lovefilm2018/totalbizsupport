# Content Strategy & Organic Tactics — TotalBiz Support

## Core Content Pillars

### Pillar 1: Operational Demystification (Tier 2 Focus)
- **Concept:** Translating dense corporate frameworks (Target Operating Models, RAID logs, Agile workflows) into pragmatic advice for 5–20 person teams.
- **Hook/Angle:** *"Why 90% of small business 'growth pain' is actually just broken workflows."*
- **Primary Channels:** LinkedIn, Medium, B2B Email.

### Pillar 2: The Local "Hero" (Tier 1 Focus)
- **Concept:** Showcasing hands-on physical & digital transformations in East Sussex.
- **Hook/Angle:** Before-and-after server room cleanup, smart lock installs saving an Airbnb host at 11 PM, fixing Wi-Fi through 2-foot thick Sussex stone walls.
- **Primary Channels:** Nextdoor, Facebook Local Groups, GBP Posts.

### Pillar 3: Tech Translation & Everyday AI Hacks (Tier 1 & 2 Focus)
- **Concept:** Practical, high-value tech and AI tips for non-techy small biz owners and everyday people.
- **Viral Hooks:**
  - *"Double-tap power on Android -> share screen -> 'Fact check this Facebook post'."*
  - *"Snap your fridge -> ask Gemini for 3 dinner recipes."*
  - *"How to scan paper invoices straight into your tax software in 10 seconds."*
- **Primary Channels:** Facebook, Nextdoor, LinkedIn, YouTube Shorts / Reels (Faceless voiceovers / avatars).

### Pillar 4: The "Rolls-Royce Mechanic" Ethos (Brand Focus)
- **Concept:** Raillng against bloated agency fees (£150/hr), corporate jargon, and overcomplicated software while advocating for enterprise-grade discipline at small business prices.
- **Primary Channels:** All platforms / Brand Manifesto.
