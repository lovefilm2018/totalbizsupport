## Top-of-Funnel Viral Content Ideas & Everyday AI Life Hacks

### Hack 1: The Facebook / Social Media "Fact Checker"
- **Target Audience:** Everyday Facebook/social media users overwhelmed by fake news or celebrity rumors.
- **Pain Point:** Seeing suspicious or far-fetched viral posts.
- **The Solution:** Double-tap power key on Android ➔ bring up Gemini ➔ tap "Share Screen" ➔ type *"Fact Check this post"*. Gemini analyzes the image/text live and confirms whether it's real or fake.
- **Hook Title:** *"How to fact-check any Facebook post in 3 seconds without leaving the app."*

### Hack 2: The "What's in My Fridge?" Meal Generator
- **Target Audience:** Busy professionals, parents, sole traders coming home tired.
- **Pain Point:** Not knowing what to cook with random ingredients in the fridge.
- **The Solution:** Open Gemini app ➔ tap camera icon ➔ snap a photo of the inside of the fridge ➔ ask *"Give me 3 quick dinner ideas using only these ingredients."*
- **Hook Title:** *"Stop throwing away food: How AI creates dinner from your fridge photo."*

### Hack 3: Instant Invoice & Tax Receipt Scanner
- **Target Audience:** Local sole traders, plumbers, builders, tradespeople.
- **Pain Point:** Paper receipts crumpling in the van / tax season panic.
- **The Solution:** Snap paper receipt ➔ AI extracts vendor, date, VAT, total ➔ pushes straight to digital bookkeeping software.
- **Hook Title:** *"How Sussex tradespeople reclaim 5 hours a week from receipt paperwork."*
